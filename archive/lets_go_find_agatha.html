Let's go find Agatha

Summary

What happens after Billy steps through the portal to go to Tommy.

As Billy stepped through the portal, Agatha chuckled. "Told him the cat was a fluke." She turned away as the portal closed behind him and floated through the wall and back to the study. There, books were scattered across every available surface. She shoved the ones on top to the floor. Billy can clean them up when he gets back. He's the one that had dragged out every book he could find about portals anyway. Time to get back to her research on transfiguration and transmutation.

She had grown accustomed to her ghostly form over the last couple of years. Walking through walls and apparating out of nowhere was a fun party trick, and she quite enjoyed how Teen still jumped every single time. And she'd gotten fairly adept at interacting with the physical world. But goddammit, she wanted a body back. A real body, flesh and blood and good hair. Her old body, preferably. Or a close approximation.

Agatha knows corporeality is achievable, especially with Teen's inherited reality manipulation. She just had to figure out how. For whatever reason, he still hasn't quite mastered doing it consciously. What is it with these Maximoffs only being able to harness their powers properly subconsciously? Drama queens. They really needed more formal training. Or therapy. Or both.

So Agatha picked up where she left off with some diary from a 15th century Bohemian witch. Before being betrayed by her village and offered up to witch hunters, she had become quite adept at transfiguration and there were a few rituals in there that seemed promising.

Agatha started mumbling to herself. "This witch might've been onto something. God, I hope that boy isn't off getting himself killed. That would be so inconvenient."

"Yes, it would be a real shame, wouldn't it?"

Agatha whipped around at the sound of a voice she never thought she would hear again. It was so surprising to her that even after centuries of projecting nonchalance in the most tense of situations, the shock was momentarily very apparent on her face. She pushed the panic down as quickly as she could.

"Wanda? How lovely to see you, dear. What brings you back to Westview? I had heard you'd skedaddled off this plane of existence. And wouldn't you know it? You literally just missed seeing Billy."

"I am aware, Agatha. That's why I'm here."

Huh. Now that was weird and gave Agatha pause. The Wanda she knew would have flipped out knowing one of her boys had survived the collapse of the hex. Would’ve moved heaven and earth and a whole town’s reality to see them again. Something wasn't right.

"Oh? Well you're welcome to make yourself at home and wait for him, hon. If you're lucky, he'll come back with Toby in tow."

Wanda smiled a cold, unsettling smile. "I'm not here for them, Agatha."

Ok, that is definitely not good. Whatever the ghost equivalent of the hair standing up on the back of your neck is, that's what Agatha felt in that moment. She could feel the fight or flight kicking in and this time, she chose flight and tried to apparate away, only to find she couldn't.

"Nuh uh uh, Agatha," Wanda said, waggling her finger at her. "I've been sent to help you."

"Ohhh aren’t you a sweetheart. But that's ok hon," she said, again trying to sound as casual as possible. "Billy's been doing a bang up job in his studies and we’re so close to a breakthrough. I'd hate to pull the rug out from under him whe..."

Wanda held up a hand. "Stop talking."

Agatha found herself unable to continue and worse...unable to move as she saw crimson energy beginning to glow around Wanda's hands. The panic had fully set in now and she began to feel...something.

Starting from the core of her ghostly form, a weird tingling sensation began. It kinda tickled at first, but then it grew. It wasn't pain, per se. In fact, it wasn't dissimilar from the heat she felt when being hit by a witch’s blast. It began to spread through her chest and out to her extremities.

She felt...heavy. She felt...holy shit. She FELT. Agatha looked down at herself and saw that she was no longer transparent. She was solid. Tangible. Alive.

She realized she could move again and ran her hands across her stomach, smoothing the fabric of the robes that Wanda had put her in. Not her style, mind you, but better than being naked she supposed, and at least they were purple. She pulled at her magic and felt it rise in the palm of her hand, a small comfort.

'Wanda, how? Why?" For once, she found herself at a total loss for words.

"You told me I'd need you. And you were right. We do."

"We? Who is…"

Then Wanda raised her hand again, interrupting her. "Part of me is sorry to have to do this again, Agatha, but..."

And then Agatha began to feel herself slipping away again.

"Wanda, no! Wait! No please not again, I can't lose another thr..."

And then, silence.

There Agatha stood with a smirk on her face, all hints of panic gone in an instant.

"Harbingers of Doom, then?"

Wanda smiled that unsettling smile once more and with a wave of her hand, they both disappeared.

— A few hours later…

A portal opens and Billy and Tommy step through and into the house.

All smiles, Billy yells, "Agathaaaa! We're back! Please don't jump out and try to scare us!"

They look around and see the books strewn across the floor and Billy’s smile droops a little.

"Agatha? Are you here?" Billy turns to Tommy. "I don't understand. She knew I would be coming back as soon as I could."

Tommy shrugs. "Maybe she got bored and wandered off to haunt something? I can't believe Agn...Agatha is your weird imaginary friend."

"I told you she's not imagina..."

"WHERE IS SHE, ABOMINATIONS?!"

The twins spin around and Tommy screams as he takes in the imposing figure of Death standing there in front of them in all of her skull-faced glory. Billy just gulps and looks nervous.

"Rio...er...Ms Death, ma'am. I don't know. We just got here."

Rio looks around, expression furious and - had Billy not been too busy trying to not shit his too tight emo pants to notice - afraid.

"This place reeks of chaos magic, abomination #1, but it's not yours."

Billy's eyes widen. "Chaos magic? But wouldn't that mean it's our m...Wanda?"

"Yes. The Scarlet Bitch was here but something's not right. The magic is...tainted somehow. It tastes wrong." She pauses, face hardening. "She's taken Agatha."

Tommy still stood there looking completely out of his element. "But why? Why would they leave if they knew we were coming back? Where would she take her? And ho..."

Rio whips out her dagger and Tommy freezes. She slices through reality and steps a foot into the void. She looks back for a moment and says, "To Doom. Go now, abominations. Seek out America and the little Marvel." She steps through the portal and it seals up behind her.

Billy and Tommy look at each other in complete confusion and fear.

Tommy speaks first. "Billy, who the fuck was that?"

Billy sighs. "That was Agatha's wife, Rio. She’s Death, like with a capital D. It's a long story."

Slack-jawed, Tommy simply says, "Death? I thought she was married to some guy named Ralph."

"Like I said. Long story."

"Ok, well what did she mean about seeking out America? Aren't we in America?"

Billy shrugs and said, "I have no idea but I might know who the little Marvel is. I guess that's where we should start."

They are silent for another moment, soaking it all in, until a little brown and white bunny hops into the room and thumps the floor.

Billy reaches down and scoops him up, scratching him between the ears. "Don't worry, buddy. We won't leave you here alone." He looks at Tommy. "I guess…we should probably go?"

Tommy looks around and then zooms over to the door, opening it. "Ok then. Let's go find Agatha."
